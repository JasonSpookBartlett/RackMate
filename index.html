Setting up a match

Enter the two player names, choose 8-Ball, 9-Ball or 10-Ball, then set the match length. Race To and Best Of stay linked automatically. Choose Alternate Break or Winner Break, then choose Lag, Coin Toss or Manual for the opening break.

Choosing the first break

Lag: lag on the real table, then choose the player who won. Manual: choose the first breaker directly. Coin Toss: tap the coin to flip it, then drag it onto the player who won the verbal Heads/Tails call. Releasing the coin starts Frame 1.

Keeping score

Swipe upward anywhere on a player’s half to award that player a frame. Swipe downward on a player’s half to remove that player’s most recently credited frame. On a computer, click and drag upward or downward with the mouse in the same way.

Undo

Undo reverses the most recent scoring action and restores the previous score, frame number, breaker and Hill state.

The break

The white cue ball shows who breaks the current frame. Alternate Break changes breaker every frame. Winner Break gives the next break to the previous frame winner.

Match display

FRAME X OF Y shows the frame currently being played and the maximum possible frames. At 6–3 in a Race to 7, RackMate shows FRAME 10 OF 13.

On the Hill

ON THE HILL means one more frame is needed to win. If both players need one, RackMate shows HILL-HILL MATCH.

Edit Match Settings

Change player names, match length, break format or current breaker without wiping the score. RackMate warns if a new Race To conflicts with the current score.

Winning the match

The winner screen shows the final score, game type and actual number of frames played, plus a cheeky RackMate message.

Rematch or new match

Rematch keeps the current settings and resets the score. New Match returns to setup.
