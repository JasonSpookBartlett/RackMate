RackMate Help

WELCOME TO RACKMATE

RackMate is a simple pool match scorer for two players at the table. Set up your race, decide the opening break, then let RackMate keep track of the score and who breaks next.

RackMate is designed for social matches - not tournaments, leagues or paperwork. Set it up, put the phone down beside the table and play pool.

SETTING UP A MATCH

Enter the two player names, choose the game, set the match length and select the break format.

Game

Choose 8-Ball, 9-Ball or 10-Ball by tapping the required object ball.

Race To / Best Of

Race To is the number of frames a player must win to win the match. Best Of shows the equivalent maximum number of frames.

Changing either value automatically updates the other. Best Of must be an odd number of 3 or higher.

Break Format

  • Alternate Break - the break alternates between players after every completed frame, regardless of who won it.
  • Winner Break - the winner of the previous frame breaks the next frame.

CHOOSING THE OPENING BREAK

Choose one of the three opening methods at the bottom of Setup. Tapping the method starts the match.

Lag

Lag at the table as normal. On the 0-0 gameplay screen, drag the white cue ball from the centre to the player who won the lag. RackMate will use that player as the first breaker.

Before the first frame is scored, you can drag the cue ball to the other player if the opening breaker was assigned incorrectly.

Coin Toss

Call Heads or Tails verbally, then tap the coin to toss it. Once the result is shown, drag the coin to the player who won the toss.

RackMate then assigns that player as the opening breaker. Before the first frame is scored, the breaker can still be corrected if necessary.

Just Play

Don't care who breaks first? Choose Just Play.

RackMate starts at 0-0 with a neutral cue ball and deliberately does not record who broke Frame 1. Once Frame 1 is completed, RackMate takes over the breaker sequence from Frame 2 according to the selected break format.

SCORING A FRAME

Each player's half of the table is a scoring area.

  • Swipe UP on a player's side to award that player the frame.
  • Swipe DOWN on a player's side to remove the most recently credited frame when appropriate.

The score changes immediately. RackMate then updates the frame number, hill status and next breaker.

On a computer during testing, click-dragging up or down performs the same actions. Double-clicking a player's side can also award a frame.

THE BREAKER INDICATOR

The white cue ball in the centre area shows who should break the next frame.

  • Alternate Break - RackMate switches the breaker after each completed frame.
  • Winner Break - RackMate moves the breaker to the winner of the previous frame.

After Frame 1 has been scored, the normal breaker sequence is locked to the match history.

UNDO

UNDO reverses the most recent scoring action and restores the match to the state it was in before that frame was entered. This includes the score, frame number, hill status and breaker.

If Undo returns a Lag or Coin Toss match to 0-0, the original opening breaker is retained. You do not repeat the lag or coin toss.

In a Just Play match, Undoing Frame 1 back to 0-0 restores the neutral centre cue because RackMate did not record the original Frame 1 breaker.

EDIT MATCH SETTINGS

Tap EDIT MATCH SETTINGS during a match if something needs correcting.

You can change:

  • Player names
  • Race To / Best Of
  • Break format
  • Current breaker

The existing score and match history are preserved.

RackMate will not allow the Race To value to be reduced to a number that is already reached or exceeded by the current score.

The game type and original opening method cannot be changed once the match has started.

MATCH MENU

Open the match menu when you need to pause, concede or abandon the current match.

Resume

Closes the menu and returns to the match.

Player Concedes

Use P1 CONCEDES or P2 CONCEDES when a player concedes the match. The current score is preserved and RackMate records the other player as the winner by concession.

Abandon Match

Use ABANDON MATCH when the match is being stopped without a result.

An abandoned match records no winner and discards the current score and match history. RackMate asks for confirmation before abandoning the match.

HILL AND HILL-HILL

When a player needs one more frame to win, RackMate displays ON THE HILL.

When both players need one more frame, RackMate displays HILL-HILL MATCH. The next frame decides the match.

WINNING THE MATCH

When a player reaches the Race To target, scoring stops and RackMate displays the Winner screen.

From there you can choose:

  • Rematch - play the same match setup again. A Lag or Coin Toss match performs a fresh opening Lag or Coin Toss. Just Play starts again with a neutral opening cue.
  • New Match - return to Setup with fresh player-name fields and your saved default match settings.

APP SETTINGS

Tap the gear icon on the Setup screen to open RackMate Settings.

RackMate Settings

  • Banter - turns RackMate's smart-arse comments on or off.
  • Voice Banter - when Banter is on, optionally reads RackMate's speech bubbles aloud using the device voice.
  • Sound - turns RackMate sound effects on or off.
  • Haptic - turns supported phone vibration feedback on or off.
  • Keep Screen Awake - attempts to prevent the phone from sleeping while a match is active.
  • Frame Win Animation - turns the planned frame-win ball animation on or off.

Default Match Setup

You can also choose the default game, Race To, break format, opening method and score-removal method used when starting a new match.

RESTORE SYSTEM DEFAULTS returns RackMate's settings to their original values.

SCORE REMOVAL

RackMate can remove a score in one of two ways, selected in Settings:

  • Direct - the score is removed immediately.
  • Confirm - RackMate asks for confirmation before removing the score.

BANTER, SOUND AND HAPTICS

RackMate has a personality, but you don't have to live with it.

Banter can be switched off without affecting normal match information. Voice Banter sits underneath Banter and can read the same speech bubble aloud. Sound and haptic feedback can also be disabled independently in Settings.

KEEPING THE SCREEN AWAKE

When Keep Screen Awake is enabled, RackMate requests that supported devices keep the display awake while you are using the app. Device and browser power-saving rules may still override this in some circumstances.

LANDSCAPE MODE

RackMate is designed to be used in landscape orientation. Turn the phone sideways before playing for the intended table layout and controls.

EXITING RACKMATE

EXIT APP is available from the menu on the Setup and Winner screens.

When RackMate is running as an installed web app, Android or the browser may not allow RackMate itself to forcibly close the app. If necessary, simply leave or close RackMate using the normal phone controls.

QUICK MATCH CHECK

Before the first break, have a quick look at the screen:

  • Correct player names?
  • Correct game?
  • Correct Race To?
  • Alternate Break or Winner Break?
  • Correct opening-break method?

Then put the phone somewhere safe, preferably where an enthusiastic cue, ball or beer can't test its impact resistance.

THAT'S IT

RackMate keeps score. You play pool.

And if the score is wrong, RackMate will naturally assume it was operator error.
